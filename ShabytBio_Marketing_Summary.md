# ShabytBio — Marketing Summary (for NIS students)

## The problem

Summative prep usually means re-reading slides you already sat through in class, hoping you catch the details that will actually show up on the test. It's slow, it's easy to miss things, and by the time you realize what you don't know, it's the night before.

## What ShabytBio does

Upload your lesson slides, notes, or PDF — ShabytBio (built specifically around the NIS biology summative format) turns it into a complete study package in about a minute:

- **Restructured study notes** with key terms bolded, so you can skim instead of reread
- **Hidden Details** — the specific facts and exceptions that commonly trip people up on summatives, each with a quick memory trick
- **Key Concepts** and **Likely Summative Topics**, so you know what actually matters before you open your notes
- **Test Yourself** — 10 to 16 self-test questions generated straight from your material, for real active recall instead of just rereading
- **A Readiness Checklist** to tick off before you walk into the exam
- **Visual diagrams** for the sequences and processes that are easier to see than read
- **An audio study conversation** — a two-voice spoken walkthrough of the material, for anyone who studies better by listening (on your commute, during a break, whatever)
- **A review system that remembers what you've mastered** — questions you've gotten right stop showing up as often, questions you're shaky on come back sooner, so studying gets smarter the more you use it across the semester

## Why it's actually useful, not just another AI tool

It's not a generic AI summarizer. It's built around how NIS summatives specifically work — checking whether material actually covers what it claims to, surfacing exam-style hidden details, and mirroring the way NIS teaches self-assessment. You can also tell it what to focus on ("I already know the vocab, focus on the calculations") so it adapts to exactly what you personally need instead of giving everyone the same generic output.

## Why it's fast even under exam-week pressure

If someone in your class already uploaded the exact same slide deck, you get your results instantly — no waiting. Even if a whole class is studying and uploading at the same time the night before a summative, the system queues requests automatically instead of throwing errors, so it stays reliable exactly when you need it most.
