    How does this work?

    Streak — Counts any day you upload new material or review questions — both count as studying. Missing a day resets the count, but nothing you've learned is lost, and your best streak is always shown so you can beat it again.

    Mastered — A question is marked mastered once you've answered it correctly 4 separate times, spaced out over at least 3 weeks. That spacing is what makes it stick in long-term memory rather than short-term recall — mastered questions then show up for review less and less often.
